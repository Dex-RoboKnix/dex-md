# Use case 8: A home move

*Illustrative, not a real user.*

**Profile.** A person weighing a move to another town: listings, costs, notes on schools and commutes, and a partner with a say.

**Folder kind.** Blank-with-context: saved notes and links.

## Why-ladder (invented answers)

| Rung | Answer |
|---|---|
| 6 Pattern | "We keep talking about moving and never get past the first listing." |
| 5 Procedure | "Decide whether to move, and where." |
| 4 Process | "Compare options against what we need." |
| 3 Policy | "We both have to agree." |
| 2 Principles | "Do not stretch the budget." |
| 1 Values | "A calm home life." |

## Slant

Personal decision: pros, cons and a choice to make. Wrong if the decision is already made and only planning is left (then switch to Planner at a checkpoint). The lens reads Success Criteria as "what makes us say yes".

## Sort and first grill angles

Stage 3 first sorts anything from an older version or another system (keep, where, archive). Then `dex-grill` works the gaps through the done, rules, people and inventory angles (method section 5).

## Privacy notes

- Addresses, finances and family details stay out of the vault where a general note will do.
- This is not financial or legal advice. For mortgages or contracts, Dex maps and points to an adviser.

## What done looks like

A list of options with the person's criteria, a written test for yes or no, the open questions only the two of them can answer, and issues for the next steps.
