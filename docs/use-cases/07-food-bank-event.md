# Use case 7: A community food bank event

*Illustrative, not a real user.*

**Profile.** A volunteer organiser planning a collection day with helpers, venue bookings and donor lists in spreadsheets and emails.

**Folder kind.** Blank-with-context: lists and booking confirmations, no code.

## Why-ladder (invented answers)

| Rung | Answer |
|---|---|
| 6 Pattern | "Last time two volunteers turned up for one slot and another slot was empty." |
| 5 Procedure | "Everyone knows their slot and nothing is double-booked." |
| 4 Process | "Helpers sign up, then I confirm by message." |
| 3 Policy | "Nobody works alone." |
| 2 Principles | "Volunteers' time is respected." |
| 1 Values | "Neighbours looking after neighbours." |

## Slant

Planner: bookings, lists and dates for an event. Wrong if the real problem is funding. The lens reads Constraints as "venue hours and helper numbers".

## Sort and first grill angles

Stage 3 first sorts anything from an older version or another system (keep, where, archive). Then `dex-grill` works the gaps through the people, timeline, done and inventory angles (method section 5).

## Privacy notes

- Helper and donor contact details are not copied into the vault. Role labels are used.
- Nothing about anyone's circumstances. The vault stays local.

## What done looks like

A timeline of the day with dates as stated, a roles list, a definition of done, and issues for open tasks. The organiser has confirmed the slant.
