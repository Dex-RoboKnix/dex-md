# Use cases

Eight worked examples of dex.md. Each gives the profile, the folder kind, the why-ladder filled in (see `../slant-flow.md`), the slant, five skills, privacy notes and what done looks like. Skill IDs are from `method/05-skill-library.md`.

| # | Case | Folder kind | Slant | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `01-legal-aid.md` Legal Aid cases (generic) | blank-with-context | Dispute or defence (regulated) | real-world |
| 2 | `02-dexroboknix-whole-repo.md` DexRoboKnix, one Folio for the whole repo | in-flight | Technical audit | real-world |
| 3 | `03-chat-export-database.md` AI chat-export database | blank-with-context | Research and synthesis desk | real-world |
| 4 | `04-blank-project.md` A brand-new blank project | blank | App development factory (provisional) | real-world |
| 5 | `05-dexroboknix-single-vault.md` DexRoboKnix, one Folio for one vault | in-flight | Operations | illustrative |
| 6 | `06-thesis.md` A part-written thesis | in-flight | Research and synthesis desk | illustrative |
| 7 | `07-food-bank-event.md` A community food bank event | blank-with-context | Planner | illustrative |
| 8 | `08-home-move.md` A home move | blank-with-context | Personal decision | illustrative |

Cases 5 to 8 are marked "Illustrative, not a real user". Cases 2 and 5 show two configurations of the same repo: one folio over everything, or one folio per vault.

All L2 products are pre-1.0 prototypes. Nothing here claims a shipped product.
